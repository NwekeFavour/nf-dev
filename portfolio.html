How I work 03 / Approach

A product isn't finished when it ships — it's finished when someone uses it and someone else hears about it. I stay close to both ends.

01

Build for the real constraint. Nigerian-market products live and die on payment reliability, low bandwidth, and trust — I design for that from the first commit.

02

Ship, then measure. I'd rather have something live and imperfect than perfect and unreleased — iteration beats prediction.

03

Write the words myself. Onboarding emails, promo copy, landing pages — the person who built the feature usually explains it best.
